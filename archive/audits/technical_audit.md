## Executive Summary

1. **Architecture and training claims match implementation** with high confidence. All ~50 specific technical claims in §3.1–3.2 are supported by exact lines of code. No mismatches in the model class, transformer block structure, embeddings, optimizer, schedule, loss, sampling loop, or CFG.

2. **The contrastive retrieval metrics (R@1=0.554 / R@5=0.872 / R@10=0.934 / MRR=0.689) cannot be verified.** The history file `checkpoints/contrastive_scibert_chebi20_projonly.history.json` does **not exist** in the repo (only the `.pt` file does). The numbers in the paper are unverifiable from current artifacts. The `chembl.history.json` (the *resume_ckpt* baseline, before ChEBI-20 fine-tuning) shows different numbers: R@1=0.584, R@5=0.831, R@10=0.892, MRR=0.695 — the paper''s claim is plausible as a *post-fine-tuning improvement* but cannot be confirmed.

3. **Two minor numerical discrepancies in auxiliary docs.** (a) The paper''s 23% exclusion rate matches 33,010→25,204 arithmetic (23.6%), but `outputs/truncation_analysis.md` reports 17.6% from a different baseline (~30,587 instead of 33,010). The paper''s claim is the defensible one. (b) The paper''s 28,274,192 trainable parameter count is off by ~1,520 vs. a direct state_dict sum (28,275,712) — within counting-convention tolerance, effectively MATCH.

4. **Dependency-aware decoding scope is narrower than the paper suggests.** Paper §5.5 says it "modifies the MaskGIT confidence-ordering rule so that ring-count tokens (immediately following a `[RingN]` opener) are deferred". Code (`_compute_not_ready_mask`, lines 480–518) implements this *only* with the additional precondition that *some earlier position is still masked* — the rule is *conditional* deferral, not unconditional. Paper wording is technically accurate but reads as if all ring-count tokens are deferred; readers may misunderstand.

5. **Probe methodology (G4) MATCHES.** Both mode-1 (one-at-a-time) and mode-2 (all-at-once-per-category) are explicitly implemented in `scripts/probe_token_knowledge.py:273–314`.
